By Pink Punk (Sandra Jõgeva, Kristin Kalamees, Margus Tamm)
Video, 2003

“Art Hotline” was an art phone line modelled on sex hotlines and their aesthetics. Calls to 055 62 62 62 were answered by Kristin, Sandra and Krista. Callers could ask questions about art, but just as important was the chance to voice their own thoughts and frustrations about contemporary art.

The hotline was launched on 30 April 2003 at the strip club X-Club. The promotional flyer was designed by Margus Tamm.

Digitised from VHS by the Estonian Centre for Contemporary Art.
