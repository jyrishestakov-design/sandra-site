**Voice of God / His Master’s Voice**
2007
Based on a recording of speaking in tongues, a religious practice widespread in many Christian congregations known for their fanaticism. Subtitles in the International Phonetic Alphabet have been added.
This video was used in a series of participatory performances of the same title in 2007–2009 in Berlin, New York, Alytus (Lithuania) and Tartu (Estonia).
