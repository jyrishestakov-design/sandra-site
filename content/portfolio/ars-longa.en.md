For the solo exhibition of the same title at Tallinn City Gallery in 2007. For 7 months in 2007, the installing and taking down of different exhibitions of the main hall of Tallinn Art Hall was documented by Jaan Klyszejko (Jaan Klõsheiko), a well-known Estonian photographer famous for documenting many Estonian art events since the 60s.
