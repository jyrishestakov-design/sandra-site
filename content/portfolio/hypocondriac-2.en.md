---
title: Hypochondriac
date: 2011-01-01
---

"Hypochondriac" is part of a performance by nine female artists (Sandra Jõgeva, Mai Sööt, Tiina Sööt, Alla Ting, Epp Kubu and Olivia Verrev from Estonia, Julischka Stengele from Germany, Teresa Novotny and Cizzy Conzales from Austria) titled "How to properly love/destroy something". It was performed at LABfactory in Vienna, Friedrichstadt Zentral in Dresden and in Flutgraben in Berlin in March 2011. An additional performance was given at the Culture Factory Festival in Polymer in Tallinn in August 2011.
