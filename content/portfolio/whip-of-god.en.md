Diverse Universe III, Lahti, Finland
The performance was about 15 minutes long and dealt with the extreme ecstasy of both S/M and belonging to an obscure religious organization. It included me telling my story of being a dominatrix for 1.5 years, offering the audience a possibility to receive a free public S/M session (which nobody wanted), using the sound of the religious practice called "speaking in tongues" and "smoking out the audience" at the end of the performance. It was conceived as a forerunner to stand-up, comparing belonging to a sect with secret sadomasochistic tendencies.
