## Malle Leis Estate

[![Malle Leis Estate – malleleis.ee](/images/malle-leis-estate.webp)](https://malleleis.ee/en)

The Malle Leis Estate is run by the artist's heirs, Sandra and Henrik Jõgeva. The Estate preserves Malle Leis' creative legacy, presents the Malle Leis Award to a woman artist every five years, and runs an artist residency in Molini di Triora, Liguria, Italy. Its online shop offers giclée prints and silk scarves based on Malle Leis' serigraphs and watercolours.
